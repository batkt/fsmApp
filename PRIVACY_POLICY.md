# Privacy Policy for workEase

**Effective Date: March 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **workEase** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Data
While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). This includes:
* Employee ID or Username
* Contact Information (Email/Phone)
* Profile Information

### Biometric Data
The App provides an option to use biometric authentication (Fingerprint or Face ID) for secure login. 
* **Local Processing:** Biometric data is stored and processed exclusively on your device. 
* **No Transmission:** We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your biometric data to our servers. The App only receives a success/failure confirmation from the operating system's secure authentication service.

### Images and Media
If you use features that involve task documentation (e.g., uploading progress photos), the App will request access to your device's camera or photo library. These images are only used for the specific task you are updating.

## 2. Permissions and Device Data

The App requires the following permissions to function correctly:
* **Internet Access:** To synchronize tasks and chat messages with our servers.
* **Notifications:** To alert you about new task assignments, status updates, and chat messages via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
* **Vibration:** For tactile feedback during notifications or app interactions.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use **Google Firebase** for core infrastructure:
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** To deliver real-time push notifications.
* **Firebase Authentication:** To manage secure access to your account.
Google may collect certain device identifiers (such as Android ID or Advertising ID) as part of these services. For more information, please visit [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Data Safety and Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, and we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Account Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of their accounts and associated data. To request account deletion, please contact your organization administrator or use the support contact provided below.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
